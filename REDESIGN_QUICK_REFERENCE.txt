🎯 DESIGN STANDARDS
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Colors:
  • Always use cyan (#00D9FF) for primary interactions
  • Green (#00FF88) for gains/positive
  • Pink (#FF3860) for losses/negative
  • Gray (#8FA3C0) for muted secondary text
  • Never use bright yellow or jarring colors

Typography:
  • Minimum font size: 16px (body text)
  • Headings: 22px+
  • Card values: 26px+
  • Always use tracking-wider for labels

Spacing:
  • Minimum 24px padding in cards
  • 6-unit gap (24px) between sections
  • 12-16px button padding
  • 20px height for nav items (80px total)

Interactions:
  • All transitions: 300-500ms
  • Hover effects: Border glow + shadow
  • Active states: Cyan color + drop shadow
  • Loading: Smooth spin animation
